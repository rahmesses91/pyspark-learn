Hi Rahul,

Congratulations on making it to the final round of interviews at Chewy! The team is excited to meet with you. Below are some helpful tips to guide your preparation:

Interview Format

Expect a mix of behavioral and technical questions. Behavioral questions are based on Chewy’s Operating Principles, which guide how we make decisions and approach challenges. You’ll often hear prompts like:

“Tell me about a time when…”
“Describe a situation where…”
“Explain how you handled…”
Interviewers are looking for detailed examples of how you’ve approached past situations, with a focus on your individual contributions.

Preparation Tips

Review Chewy’s Operating Principles: You don’t need to memorize them, but prepare 2–3 examples for each. This helps avoid repeating stories across interviewers.
Use the STAR Method: Structure your answers using Situation, Task, Action, Result. Here’s a helpful video on STAR.
Be Concise: Aim for 2–3 minutes per response.
Focus on Your Role: Use “I” statements and highlight your direct contributions.
Ask Clarifying Questions: If anything is unclear, it’s perfectly fine to ask.
Have Questions Ready: Thoughtful questions show your engagement and interest.
Prepare for “Why Chewy?” or “Why This Role?”: Go beyond being a pet lover—show your alignment with our mission and the role’s impact.
Research Interviewers: Check out their LinkedIn profiles if available. I’m happy to help if you need assistance.
“Operate at Depth”: Be ready to discuss how you’ve used data to drive decisions in past roles.
If you have any questions ahead of the interview, don’t hesitate to reach out. Best of luck—you’ve got this!
