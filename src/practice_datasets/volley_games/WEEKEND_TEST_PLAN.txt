--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. POSTGRESQL VS MYSQL (quick mapping)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - DATE(col)           → (col)::date or DATE_TRUNC('day', col)::date
  - TIMESTAMPDIFF(MINUTE,a,b) → EXTRACT(EPOCH FROM (b-a))/60 or (b-a)/INTERVAL '1 minute'
  - DATE_ADD(d, INTERVAL 7 DAY) → d + INTERVAL '7 days'
  - WEEKDAY(d)          → EXTRACT(DOW FROM d) (Sun=0) or use DATE_TRUNC('week', d)
  - ROUND(x,2)          → ROUND(x::numeric, 2)
  - JSON_EXTRACT        → event_data->>'recognized' or event_data->'recognized'
  - No USE db;          → \c or connect in client; schema prefix optional.
